# SHACL shapes for OQUO evaluation results

[`oquo-shapes.ttl`](./oquo-shapes.ttl) contains [SHACL](https://www.w3.org/TR/shacl/) shapes for validating evaluation results described with OQUO, such as the RDF generated by [oquo-instance-creator](https://github.com/tecnomod-um/oquo-instance-creator) and [oquo-instance-creator-endpoint](https://github.com/tecnomod-um/oquo-instance-creator-endpoint).

The OWL axioms of OQUO describe what can be inferred from the data, but they cannot check that the data are complete: for example, that every evaluation has exactly one time instant or that every quality value has a value and a scale. The shapes check that each resource of an evaluation result has the properties it requires, with the right number of values of the right type, and that it has no unexpected properties.

## How to validate

The shapes must be applied to the evaluation results **together with the OQUO ontologies they use, and with OWL inference**, because:

- `sh:targetClass` also selects the instances of subclasses only if the data graph contains the `rdfs:subClassOf` axioms. For example, evaluations are validated by `ProvActivityShape` because `eval:Evaluation` is a subclass of `prov:Activity` in the core.
- Some constraints rely on inferred types and properties. For example, `sh:class qm:QualityMeasure` is satisfied by a metric such as `oquo-huron:NamesPerClassMetric` only if its module is loaded, and `ipo:hostAssetOf` is the inverse of the `ipo:hasHostAsset` given in the data.

With [pySHACL](https://github.com/RDFLib/pySHACL):

```bash
pip install pyshacl

pyshacl --shacl SHACL/oquo-shapes.ttl \
        --ont-graph oquo-huron.owl --imports \
        --inference owlrl \
        --format human \
        results.ttl
```

- `--ont-graph` is the OQUO module of the framework the results come from (here, HURON). With `--imports`, its imports (the quality model and the OQUO core) are loaded too.
- `--inference owlrl` applies OWL RL inference to the data and the ontologies before validating.
- `--format human` prints a readable report; use `turtle` to get the report as RDF.

`pyshacl` exits with code `0` if the data conform to the shapes, and with code `1` otherwise.

## Shapes

All the constraints have severity `sh:Violation`. A **closed** shape only accepts the properties it declares, besides `rdf:type` and those listed in `sh:ignoredProperties`. An **open** shape only checks the properties it declares and allows any others.

### Evaluation results

| Shape | Target class | Closed | Constraints |
|---|---|:-:|---|
| `oquo:EvaluationShape` | `eval:Evaluation` | yes | Exactly one `eval:evaluatedSubject` (an `eval:EvaluationSubject` and `oboe:Entity`), one `oquo:hasObservation` (`oboe:Observation`), one `time:hasTime` (`time:Instant`) and one `eval:producedQualityValue` (`eval:QualityValue`). At least one `eval:inputData` (`eval:EvaluationData`). Any number of `oquo:hasDetectedIssue` (`ipo:Issue`) and `prov:generated`, and at most one `prov:wasAssociatedWith`. |
| `oquo:InstantShape` | `time:Instant` | yes | Exactly one `time:inXSDDateTime`, of type `xsd:dateTime`. |
| `oquo:QualityValueShape` | `eval:QualityValue` | yes | Exactly one `eval:hasLiteralValue`, one `eval:isMeasuredOnScale` (`om:Measurement_scale`) and one `eval:forMeasure` (`qm:QualityMeasure`). At least one `eval:obtainedFrom` (`eval:Evaluation`). Optional `rdfs:comment`, at most one `prov:wasGeneratedBy`, any number of `prov:wasAttributedTo`, at most one `obo:IAO_0000136` (*is about*, an `om:Measurement_scale_category`) and any number of `prov:wasDerivedFrom` (`eval:QualityValue`). |
| `oquo:MeasuredValueShape` | `oboe:MeasuredValue` | yes | Same constraints as `QualityValueShape`. Since every quality value is a measured value, both shapes apply to quality values. |

Quality values obtained by applying a scale conversion of an OQUO module to another quality value, such as those generated by the `/toOquoModel` method of oquo-instance-creator-endpoint, are validated by the same shapes. They are measured on the output scale of the conversion, and they are linked to the quality value they were converted from with `prov:wasDerivedFrom`. When the output scale is ordinal or nominal, their `eval:hasLiteralValue` is the code of the category on the scale (e.g. its `om:rank`), and they are linked to the category itself with `obo:IAO_0000136` (*is about*). They share the measure, evaluation and agents of the original quality value, but they are not values of the measurement (`oboe:hasValue`) nor the quality value produced by the evaluation (`eval:producedQualityValue`), which are restricted to one value each.

### Observations and measurements

| Shape | Target class | Closed | Constraints |
|---|---|:-:|---|
| `oquo:ObservationShape` | `oboe:Observation` | yes | Exactly one `oboe:ofEntity` (an `oboe:Entity` and `eval:EvaluationSubject`), one `oboe:hasMeasurement` (`oboe:Measurement`) and one `time:hasTime` (`time:Instant`). Any number of `ipo:indicates` (`ipo:Issue`). |
| `oquo:MeasurementShape` | `oboe:Measurement` | yes | Exactly one `oboe:measurementFor` (`oboe:Observation`) and one `oboe:hasValue` (an `oboe:MeasuredValue` and `eval:QualityValue`). At least one `qm:hasScale` (`om:Measurement_scale`). |

### Scales

| Shape | Target class | Closed | Constraints |
|---|---|:-:|---|
| `oquo:MeasurementScaleShape` | `om:Measurement_scale` | no | Exactly one `qm:hasRankigFunction`, either `qm:HigherBest` or `qm:LowerBest`. At least one definition (`obo:IAO_0000115`) and one `rdfs:label`, of type `xsd:string` or `rdf:langString` (with a language tag, e.g. `"…"@en`). |
| `oquo:OrdinalScaleShape` | `om:Ordinal_scale` | no | Same as `MeasurementScaleShape`, plus at least two `om:element` (`om:Ordered_measurement_scale_category`). |

These shapes apply to the scales defined in the OQUO modules, so they are only checked when the modules are part of the validated graph.

### Issues

| Shape | Target class | Closed | Constraints |
|---|---|:-:|---|
| `oquo:IssueShape` | `ipo:Issue` | yes | At least one `ipo:indicatedBy` (`ipo:Symptom`), one `ipo:hasHostAsset` and one `ipo:hasAsset` (`ipo:Asset`). Any number of `ipo:hasCausativeAsset` (`ipo:Asset`). Exactly one `rdfs:comment`, of type `xsd:string` or `rdf:langString`. |
| `oquo:SymptomShape` | `ipo:Symptom` | yes | At least one `ipo:indicates` (`ipo:Issue`). Exactly one `oboe:ofEntity` (an `oboe:Entity`, `eval:EvaluationSubject` and `ipo:Asset`), one `oboe:hasMeasurement` (`oboe:Measurement`) and one `time:hasTime` (`time:Instant`). |
| `oquo:AssetShape` | `ipo:Asset` | no | At least one `ipo:hostAssetOf` (`ipo:Issue`). Open, because an asset can be any entity, with any properties. |

### Provenance

| Shape | Target class | Closed | Constraints |
|---|---|:-:|---|
| `oquo:ProvActivityShape` | `prov:Activity` | no | The values of `prov:generated` are `prov:Entity`, and those of `prov:wasAssociatedWith` are `prov:Agent`. |
| `oquo:ProvEntityShape` | `prov:Entity` | no | The values of `prov:wasGeneratedBy` are `prov:Activity`, and those of `prov:wasAttributedTo` are `prov:Agent`. |

The PROV-O shapes are open, because anything can be a `prov:Activity` or a `prov:Entity`. They only check the types of the values of the PROV-O properties, while the shapes of OQUO declare which PROV-O properties evaluations and quality values can have. The agent of an evaluation must therefore be typed as a `prov:Agent` (for example, `prov:SoftwareAgent`) in the validated graph, either in the evaluation results or in the OQUO module that describes it.

`prov:wasInfluencedBy` and `prov:influenced` are ignored by the closed shapes. They are not generated, but inferred from their subproperties (`prov:wasAssociatedWith`, `prov:wasGeneratedBy`, `prov:wasAttributedTo` and `prov:generated`), so they appear whenever the data are validated with inference.

## Prefixes

```turtle
@prefix sh:    <http://www.w3.org/ns/shacl#> .
@prefix rdf:   <http://www.w3.org/1999/02/22-rdf-syntax-ns#> .
@prefix xsd:   <http://www.w3.org/2001/XMLSchema#> .
@prefix oquo:  <https://purl.org/oquo#> .
@prefix eval:  <http://purl.org/net/EvaluationResult#> .
@prefix qm:    <http://purl.org/net/QualityModel#> .
@prefix oboe:  <http://ecoinformatics.org/oboe/oboe.1.2/oboe-core.owl#> .
@prefix ipo:   <http://purl.org/ipo/core#> .
@prefix om:    <http://www.wurvoc.org/vocabularies/om-1.8/> .
@prefix time:  <http://www.w3.org/2006/time#> .
@prefix prov:  <http://www.w3.org/ns/prov#> .
@prefix obo:   <http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/> .
```

In `oquo-shapes.ttl`, the IAO terms are written with the prefix `iao:` bound to `http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/IAO_`: the definitions (`obo:IAO_0000115`) as `iao:0000115`, and *is about* (`obo:IAO_0000136`) as `iao:0000136`.
